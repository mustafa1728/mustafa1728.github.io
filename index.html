Mustafa Chasmai

CICS at UMass Amherst, Massachusetts, USA.

I am a PhD Student at UMass Amherst, working broadly in Computer Vision under Prof. Subhransu Maji. My research focuses on AI applied for multimodal ecological applications such as bioacoustics, geolocalization, and morphometrics.

Prior to this, I completed my Bachelors degree in Computer Science from IIT Delhi.
